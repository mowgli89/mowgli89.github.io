Meet the lab

The people behind the work.

We are a small group of surgeons, scientists, and trainees who care about the fetal lung and about the families who live with what happens to it. The work runs from calcium imaging at the bench to the operating room, and most of us spend time in both.

Join us

We welcome students, residents, and postdocs who are curious about fetal lung biology and mechanobiology. Reach out through the contact page.
